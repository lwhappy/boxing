Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains what information SilentJab (silentjab.com) collects when you visit, why, and what control you have over it. We keep this as short as we honestly can.

The short version

  • We don't ask you to sign up, and we don't have accounts.
  • We don't collect names, emails or personal profiles through the site itself.
  • Aggregate, anonymous visit statistics are collected by Google Analytics.
  • Advertising cookies may be used once we display ads; you can opt out at any time.

Analytics (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used — for example which pages are visited most and whether the 3D demos perform well. Google Analytics sets cookies that collect anonymous usage data such as pages viewed, approximate location (country/city level), browser type and visit duration. This data does not identify you personally and we do not attempt to re-identify anyone.

You can block Google Analytics with any standard cookie-blocking tool, or install Google's official opt-out browser add-on: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. Google's own privacy policy explains how it handles this data: policies.google.com/privacy.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

We may display ads served by Google AdSense to keep the site free. When ads are active, Google and its partners may use cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising at google.com/settings/ads.
  • You can also manage third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info/choices.

What we never collect

SilentJab has no registration, no comment forms and no newsletter. The contact page works entirely by your own email client — if you email us at contact@silentjab.com, we use your address only to reply, and we never share it or add it to any list.

Data stored in your browser

The site stores tiny preference values in your browser's local storage — for example, whether the fight-page sound is muted. These never leave your device and are not linked to you personally. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Children's privacy

SilentJab is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since we collect none at all, there is nothing to disclose — but if you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the site works in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and the date above. Questions? Email contact@silentjab.com.
