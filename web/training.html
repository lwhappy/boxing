Boxing Punches in Action — See Every Basic Punch Land in a Real Fight

A technique page shows a punch in isolation. A real fight never gives you that luxury: the target moves, the distance keeps changing, and every punch must start from your guard and finish back in it. This 3D sparring demo puts all six basic boxing punches into that practical scenario — one live defender, standing right in front of you.

Pick a punch under the stage and watch it land: where the fist meets the head, how the defender reacts to the impact, and how the attacker returns to stance before throwing the next shot. Drag to rotate the scene, scroll to zoom, and replay any punch as many times as you need.

The six punches you can throw in this demo

  • Jab — the range-finder. The fastest punch in boxing, thrown from the lead hand; it opens every exchange and lands from the longest distance.
  • Cross — the power straight from the rear hand. It follows the jab through the opening and carries the body's rotation behind it.
  • Lead Hook — a short, arcing shot that comes around the guard to the side of the jaw once the distance closes.
  • Rear Hook — the heavier arc from the back hand; ideal when the opponent slips inside your straight punches.
  • Lead Uppercut — digs under a low guard and lifts the chin when the opponent leans in close.
  • Rear Uppercut — the rear-hand version: slower to arrive, but the hardest of the six and a real fight-ender at close range.

Watching punches land is step one. Next, study each strike on its own moves page, then learn how to chain them in real combinations — a jab-cross-hook sequence behaves very differently from an uppercut finished with a hook. If you want to drill the timing yourself, the buttons above let you punch a live target with your own calls.
