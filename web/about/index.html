About SilentJab

SilentJab is a free, interactive 3D reference for the fundamentals of boxing. Instead of still photos and long paragraphs, every punch, combination and drill on this site is shown as a live 3D scene you can rotate, replay and study from any angle — with technique notes written next to it.

What you'll find here

  • Moves — the six basic punches (jab, cross, lead hook, rear hook, lead uppercut, rear uppercut), each with its own 3D demo and breakdown of action, timing, advantages and limits.
  • Combos — ten real punch combinations shown as continuous 3D sequences, from the classic jab-cross to five-punch chains.
  • Training — a live-defender demo: pick any punch and watch it land, so you can study the timing instead of imagining it.

How the demos are made

Each scene is a real-time 3D animation running in your browser. The fighters are animated with motion clips captured and cleaned up frame by frame, and every page adds its own coaching notes: what the feet are doing, where the weight sits, why the punch works, and when it doesn't. Nothing is a video — you can drag to orbit the camera and slow the action down at any moment, which is exactly what stills can't give you.

Who it's for

Beginners who want to understand what each punch actually does before stepping into a gym, and visual learners who study movement better than text. SilentJab is a reference, not a coach: no page here replaces supervised training. Read our terms of use for the details.
